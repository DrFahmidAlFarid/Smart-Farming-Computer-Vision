# 🌱 Smart Farming & Computer Vision

Research hub for **computer vision, deep learning and explainable AI in agriculture**, maintained as part of Dr. Fahmid Al Farid's academic GitHub portfolio.

## Focus
- Crop and leaf disease classification
- Precision agriculture and smart farming
- Lightweight CNN/Transformer architectures
- UAV and field-vision applications
- Agricultural IoT and intelligent monitoring
- Explainable AI for plant-health decisions

## Repository roadmap
This hub will index publication-linked code, datasets, notebooks and reproducibility material that can be shared legitimately. Substantial original projects may later move to dedicated paper-specific repositories while remaining indexed here.

## Resource organization
- **Publication-linked implementations** — code/resources associated with published work
- **Datasets** — original datasets where redistribution is permitted, otherwise canonical source links
- **Notebooks** — reproducible experiments and demonstrations
- **Models & results** — shareable checkpoints, evaluation outputs and figures

## Current status
The portfolio is being curated from the publication record. Resources are added only after the paper association and reuse/ownership status are verified.

### Research integrity & reuse
This repository is an academic research hub. Publication-linked resources may be maintained by Dr. Fahmid Al Farid, collaborators, or external co-authors. External repositories are linked to their original source and remain subject to their own licenses and ownership. A publication association does not imply ownership of a collaborator's code.
